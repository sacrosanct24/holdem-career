Hold'em Career — Privacy Policy

Effective August 2026.

Hold'em Career does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. The app makes no network connections.

Everything the app saves — your career, hand histories, statistics, and settings — is stored only on your device (and in your personal device backups, which we never see). Deleting the app deletes all of it.

The app is a simulated poker game. It involves no real money and offers no way to spend any.
